Hi! I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at **Sussex AI**, University of Sussex, supervised by
[Prof. James Knight](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=asUiHlIAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Thomas Nowotny](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=W3T-i5AAAAAJ&hl=en).
My doctoral research investigates **POSIT-enabled EventProp training** and the implementation of Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) on programmable **RISC-V FPGA SoCs**.

I received my M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from
[Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic)](https://aut.ac.ir/en/)
and my B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from [Shahrood University of Technology](https://shahroodut.ac.ir/en/).

## Research interests

- Computational Neuroscience & Neuromorphic Computing
- AI Hardware Accelerators & Reconfigurable Computing
- Low-Precision POSIT Arithmetic
- High-Level Synthesis (HLS) & SystemC
- Parallel & Distributed Systems
- Real-Time & Embedded Systems
